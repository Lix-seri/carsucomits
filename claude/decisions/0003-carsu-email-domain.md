# 0003 — Keep @carsu.edu.ph as the institutional email domain

- **Date:** 2026-09-25 · **Status:** Accepted

**Context.** The CCIS-verification requirement left the domain blank. Registration already requires `@carsu.edu.ph`.

**Decision.** Keep `@carsu.edu.ph` for every account. CCIS verification adds a student ID and a proof upload, reviewed by an admin, on top of the domain rule.

**Consequences.** A matching email proves "CSU account", not "CCIS student"; that is what the verification review is for.
